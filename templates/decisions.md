# Decision log (step 7)

The only place where a person decides. A row goes here when no source can close it: a promise AG would
make, a price, a person on stage, consent, call recording, number registration, data handling.
The "To confirm" list printed with a piece is generated from the open rows.

Rules
- `owner` is a named person who has agreed to decide. An agent is never an owner. Blank means nobody has.
- `proposed owner` is our guess, by role. Nobody at AG has agreed to own any row below.
- A row about the law or a company promise is closed only by its owner, with a date.
- A cut line goes to the killed-lines table. It comes back only through a new decision row, named in `reversed by`.
