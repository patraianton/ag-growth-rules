# AG house voice, as data

Counts come from the AG style guide of 2026-09-30 (`crm-style-guide-2026-09-30.md`), over AG's English
text. Slices: **blog** = 40 posts (2021 to 29.09.2026); **LinkedIn** = 24 posts; **YouTube** = 17 webinar
descriptions; **transcripts** = 2 launch webinars (28.10.2025, 13.03.2026); **site** = home, pricing,
GrantAI, promo pages. Where the guide gives examples but no number, no number is printed.

This file covers naming and form only; whether the product works stays CLAIMED.

Scope: `email`, `social`, `webinar` and `imo-note` are in AG's voice. `how-made` is Anton's voice: the
reader words, length and never-does rules apply, except VN1 (it may name GoHighLevel as a research subject,
`competitors-partners` A4) and VN8 (first person); section 3 exceptions do not apply to it.

## 1. Launch formula

From 40 blog posts (style guide section 4):

1. Pain, one paragraph, second person.
2. The turn: "So, we fixed it." / "X changes that." / "Two new features fix that."
3. What you can do now: 3 to 6 bullets, each starting with a verb.
4. Where to find it: the menu path.
5. The plan line: "Available on the Prime+ plan and above."
6. One call to action. At most two (trial plus webinar).

| form | AG norm (style guide) |
|---|---|
| blog post | median about 360 words |
| one-feature announcement | 60 to 360 words |
| LinkedIn post | 40 to 120 words (one exception: a first-person story at about 330) |
| press release | about 470 words |
| bullets per feature | 4 to 6 |
| features per release | 4 to 5 |
| webinar, weekly | 25 to 30 minutes, Wednesday 11 AM PT, Zoom |
| webinar, launch | 43 to 64 minutes |
| "What you'll learn" items | 4 to 6 |
| "What you'll see LIVE" cards | 3 |
| speaker line | one line per person, no biography |

A piece that runs past its row is a `filler` finding unless `decisions.md` records why.

## 7. Exception rule

A banned word or shape stays in an AG-voice piece only when this file has a row for it with:

1. the exact phrase or shape;
2. its count per slice, copied from the style guide (or "no number" with the guide's section and quoted
   examples, as VA3 and VA13);
3. the pieces it applies to.

No row, no exception. Adding a row is a rule change (`rules/README.md`, "How to change a rule").
